I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the School of Data Science, University of Virginia.

My research examines how technologies evolve, how inventors and firms search and recombine knowledge, and how these processes interact with innovation and industrial policy. I use large-scale patent, firm, and network data to study technological evolution, inventive search, and technological capabilities.

## Research Interests

- Technological evolution and structural change
- Inventive search and technological recombination
- Network science and computational social science
- Innovation and industrial policy
